3D Vector Resultant Calculator (Direction Angles)

Enter magnitude and direction angles θx and θy (degrees from +x and +y). Choose the sign of the z-direction (+ or −). The tool computes θz from the direction-cosine constraint: cos²θx + cos²θy + cos²θz = 1.
Inputs: F, θx, θy, sign(z)
Vector Magnitude θx (deg) θy (deg) sign(z) θz (deg) Fx Fy Fz
Validity check: θx and θy must satisfy cos²θx + cos²θy ≤ 1. If not, the row is highlighted and components are not computed.
Reminder: Direction angles are typically between 0° and 180°.
